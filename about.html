// 01 — The person

I've always felt everything deeply

I'm Tess Fowler, a counsellor and somatic therapist on Kaurna Land, South Australia. I see individuals and couples at The Ember Room in Norwood, in Adelaide's eastern suburbs, and online across Australia.

I've always been passionate, headstrong and full of fire. Growing up, I felt everything deeply, sometimes to my own detriment. Those early years of searching, exploring and stumbling through the chaos set me on a path of discovery that led, eventually, to this work.

For over 20 years I've worked in the performing arts as an actor, singer and writer. That work has always been about the human experience: emotions, stories and the connections that shape us. It taught me the power of vulnerability, the value of presence, and the importance of feeling emotions fully and freely. Those lessons are the foundation of how I sit with clients today.

In 2024, at 33, I completed my Master of Counselling and Psychotherapy to gain formal trauma training and broaden my scope of practice. Now I blend traditional talk-based therapy with somatic approaches, so we can reach the core of what's going on for you.

I'm an artist, a seeker and a work in progress. Life is a constant cycle of learning, unlearning and growing, and I bring that same curiosity into the room.

Acknowledgement of Country

The Ember Room operates on Kaurna Land. I acknowledge the Kaurna people as the Traditional Owners and pay my respects to Elders past and present.

Who I work with

Adults and couples, often people who look fine on the outside and feel stretched thin underneath. People who feel too much, or have learned to feel very little. Anyone who needs space to slow down and listen to their inner wisdom.

Talk, and the body

I combine body-based "bottom-up" approaches with cognitive "top-down" practices. We build your capacity to sit with discomfort long enough to listen to what's underneath, so your body and mind can start to communicate as one.

Why embers

Embers burn softly with warmth, and hold the possibility of a roaring blaze. They glow beneath the surface, where subtle shifts happen. When it seems like the end, they can catch again. And we've always sat around them with the people we love, sharing stories long into the night.

Who I see

How to refer

With your client's consent, they can book a free 15-minute call themselves, or you can email me at hello@tessfowler.com.au.
