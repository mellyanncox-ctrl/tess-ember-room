What happens in the free call

Fifteen minutes, on the phone. It's a chance to hear my voice and work out whether we'd be a good fit before you commit to anything.

  1. You pick a time

    Choose a slot in my calendar that suits you. It takes a minute or two.

  2. We talk

    At the time you booked, we talk by phone. It's a phone call, so no camera and no need to tidy the lounge room.

  3. Ask me anything

    Tell me a little about what's bringing you, as much or as little as you like, and ask whatever you want to know about how I work.

  4. You decide

    If it feels right, book a first session. If you'd like time to think, take it. You don't have to decide on the call.

If what you're after needs a different kind of support, I'll say so honestly and help you find the right person.

Your first session

Ninety minutes, in person in Norwood or on Zoom. We'll talk about what's brought you here and a bit of your story, at your pace. I may invite you to notice your breath or what's happening in your body as you speak. Every invitation is optional. Towards the end we'll talk about what you'd like from our work and how often you'd like to come.
