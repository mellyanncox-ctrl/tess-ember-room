Somatic Therapy & Counselling

Individual Counselling and Somatic Therapy sessions offer an in depth chance to discover long held beliefs and behaviours that have been established over a life time, and work with both the body and the mind to discover new ways of being that create more ease, freedom and autonomy in your life.

Couples Therapy

Relationship Counselling and Somatic Therapy Sessions offer the opportunity to address struggles within your relationship that cause tension and disconnection, and find ways to establish healthy new ways of relating to each other that result in better intimacy, connection and companionship and helps your love reignite.

$210 90mins (Initial clients are required to book the 90min for first session) $180 60mins

Experience a personally curated blend of gentle somatic therapy exercises, soothing sounds and live vocals to help your body settle into a state of deep restoration. Available Tuesday, Friday, Saturday or Sunday @ 5.30pm or 7.30pm.
