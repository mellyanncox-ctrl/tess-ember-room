Everything else you might be wondering

Cost

Do I need a GP referral?

No. Counselling isn't covered by Medicare, so you don't need a referral or a Mental Health Care Plan. You can book directly, and there's no cap on the number of sessions.

Can I claim a rebate from my health fund?

I'm registered with Bupa, so if you're a Bupa member with mental health cover (counselling), you can claim a rebate. The amount depends on your policy, so it's worth checking with your fund before your first session.

Do you offer a concession rate?

Sessions

What is somatic therapy, if I've never tried it?

Somatic simply means "of the body". A lot of our patterns, like tensing up before a hard conversation or saying yes before we've had a chance to think, show up in the body before they reach the mind. In a session we talk, as you would in any counselling. I may also invite you to notice what's happening physically as you speak: your breath, where tension sits, what shifts in your chest when a particular person comes up. Nothing is done to you. Every invitation is optional, and you can say no at any point. You don't need any experience, or to be "good at" feeling your body. We start wherever you are.

Can I see you online, or do I need to come in?

Either. I see clients in person at 43 Edward Street, Norwood, and online by Zoom. Individual and couples sessions both work either way. The body-based parts of the work are adapted for video, and the fees are the same.

How often would we meet?

Usually weekly or fortnightly, and sometimes monthly. We'll work out a rhythm that suits you and your budget, and you're never locked in.

What happens in the free 15-minute call?

It's a short phone chat, with no cost and no obligation. You can tell me a little about what's bringing you, ask me anything about how I work, and we'll both get a sense of whether we're a good fit. If I'm not the right person for what you need, I'll say so honestly and help you find someone who is.
