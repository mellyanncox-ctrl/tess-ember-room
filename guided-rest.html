// 01 — What it is

A gentle alternative to the work of therapy

Guided Rest is a one-to-one session where you get to stop. There's nothing to work through and nothing to fix. It's a space where your body can soften and settle, and a chance to gently reconnect with what's going on inside.

This isn't therapy

Guided Rest isn't therapy, and it isn't a substitute for it. There's no assessment, no treatment plan and no processing. If you're working through something that needs therapeutic support, individual therapy is the place for that.

If you're in danger, call 000. For 24/7 support, call Lifeline on 13 11 14.

Who it's for

  • You feel overwhelmed, depleted and in need of deep rest.
  • You struggle to find the time, or the discipline, to rest on your own.
  • The thought of therapy feels like too much right now, but you need respite.
  • You'd like the presence of another person to guide you into rest.
  • Massage, yoga and the like aren't really your thing.
  • You've come a long way in therapy and don't always need a "big" session.

Why it's a little different

I'm a counsellor and somatic therapist, and a professional singer-songwriter and performer with more than two decades of stage experience. Guided Rest brings those two worlds together. You'll usually only find this kind of thing in group workshops, and rarely in a 1:1 session.

Tea and an intention

We start with an optional cup of tea and time to share anything that's present for you today. From there, we gently shape an intention to hold through the session.
