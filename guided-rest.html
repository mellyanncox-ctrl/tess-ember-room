A gentle alternative to the work of therapeutic processing

Therapeutic work often asks us to explore tender parts of ourselves, revisit past wounds, and gently stretch our comfort zones.

While this work can be deeply profound and meaningful, it isn’t always easy.

In an ideal world, this work would be supported with enough restorative nourishment in our day to day lives.

Alas, as life continues to move faster, let’s be honest - most of us are not giving ourselves enough of this ‘down’ time.

The Guided Rest Session offers a gentle alternative to the work of therapeutic processing.

This experience is designed to provide:

  • deep nervous system restoration
  • space where your body can soften, settle, and restore emotional balance
  • an opportunity to gently nourish and reconnect with your internal landscape in a safe and soothing way

Who is this for?

  • Those who feel overwhelmed, depleted and in need of deep rest.
  • Those who struggle to find the time or discipline to lock in dedicated RnR moments on their own.
  • Those who might feel overwhelmed at the thought of traditional ‘therapy’ but are in need of some respite from their struggles.
  • Those who want to receive the safety and presence of another being to guide them through the process of deep rest.
  • Those who want to treat themselves to something soothing, but perhaps don’t enjoy massages, yoga, or other traditional forms of relaxation.
  • Those who have come a long way in their therapy journey, and don’t always feel the need for ‘big’ sessions but want to stay engaged and connected to themselves.

What makes this experience unique?

As a psychotherapist specialising in somatic and relational work, and a professional singer-songwriter and performer with more than two decades of stage experience, I will be bringing together two worlds that are usually seen in group workshops but rarely for 1:1 sessions.

What to expect:

The session begins with an (optional) cup of tea and time to share anything that is currently present for you - whether that’s a specific circumstance, or simply a sense of fatigue or overwhelm. From here, we will gently shape a supportive intention or message that your system can anchor into.

Then you may sit or lie down amongst the cosy cushions and blankets, and using a curated blend of somatic therapies, sounds and immersive live voice, I will weave a personalised soundscape to bring you out of the busyness of the mind and back into the quiet intelligence of the body - supporting deep rest, regulation, emotional release, and a return to balance.

You’ll be in control of your experience the entire time, while also being invited into a state of receptivity, where the body is invited to let go and given full permission to simply receive.

Seven types of rest beyond sleep

There are seven types of rest beyond sleep that are essential for restoring energy, overcoming burnout, and allowing the body and mind to recharge.

The Deep Rest Sessions are designed to gently support you across all seven:

Mental Rest

Guided awareness and embodiment offers the mind a break from over-analysis, bringing you back into the present moment.

❋

Physical Rest

Through stillness, gentle movement, and supportive positioning, the body is given permission to release tension and unwind.

❋

Emotional Rest

A space where you don’t have to perform, filter, or hold it together - allowing your internal experience to be acknowledged and felt.

❋

Sensory Rest

Soft lighting, calming sound, and minimal external stimulation create an environment where the senses can settle and reset.

❋

Spiritual Rest

Moments of stillness, connection to self and meaning, invite a deeper sense of grounding, presence, and inner alignment.

❋

Social Rest

Time away from roles, expectations, and relational demands - where you are held, without needing to give back.

❋

Creative Rest

Immersion in live voice and sound offers emotional resonance, allowing your system to receive rather than produce.

❋
