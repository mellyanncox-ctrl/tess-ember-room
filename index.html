Welcome to The Ember Room Private Practice

9 times out of 10, people don't have problems needing to be solved, they have feelings needing to be felt.

I'm Tess Fowler, a counsellor and somatic therapist (MCouns&Psych) on Kaurna Land, South Australia. Based in the leafy green suburb of Norwood, in the Eastern suburbs of Adelaide, I offer in person and online individual therapy and couples therapy sessions.

Find Your No · Live Your Yes

You say yes when you want to say no. You're constantly scanning to make sure no one's mad at you. You put everyone else's needs first, then wonder why you feel resentful and disconnected from yourself.

Why is it so hard to stop? Because for many, 'people pleasing' is the nervous system's deeply ingrained way of staying safe, connected and accepted - and it shows up in your body well before it enters your conscious mind.
