    <p>For over 20 years, I’ve had a rich and fulfilling career in the performing arts. Music and acting have always been about diving deep into the human experience - exploring emotions, stories, and the connections that shape us.</p>
    <p>I’ve always been passionate, headstrong, and full of fire. Growing up, I felt everything deeply, sometimes to my own detriment. But those early years of searching, exploring, and stumbling through the chaos set me on a path of discovery.</p>
    <p>At 18, I took myself to India and began a spiritual journey of seeking for meaning and purpose.</p>
    <p>At 19, I went to drama school and studied performing arts for 3 years.</p>
    <p>In 2024 at 33, I completed my Master’s in Counselling and Psychotherapy to gain formal trauma training and broaden my scope of practice. Now I focus on organically blending traditional talk based therapies and somatic approaches to truly reach the core of what’s going on.</p>
    <p>My time in the creative arts has taught me the transformative power of vulnerability, the value of presence, and the importance of feeling emotions fully, deeply, and freely. These lessons now form the foundation of my work as a therapist. The Ember Room has been running for nearly 2 years now and continues to grow and evolve as my work deepens and expands.</p>
    <p>I’m an artist, a seeker, and a work in progress. Life is a constant evolution of learning, unlearning, and growing - and I’m thrilled to have you here for the journey.</p>
    <p>Tess x</p>
