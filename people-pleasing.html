A month for the people pleasers

You say yes when you want to say no. You’re constantly scanning to make sure no one’s mad at you.

You put everyone else's needs first, then wonder why you feel resentful and disconnected from yourself.

Find Your No*Live Your Yes

A wholehearted Yes

Maybe you're exhausted by constantly being there for everyone else. Maybe you're just beginning to wonder whether you're living from genuine choice, or from an autopilot instinct to keep everyone happy and pleased with you.

You might love caring for people. You might genuinely want to say yes sometimes.

The question is: can you tell the difference between a wholehearted Yes and a Yes that comes at the expense of yourself? Or have the two become so muddled that you struggle to know where your No even begins?

Maybe you've practised saying no in your head, only to do exactly the same thing over again, feeling incapable of change and starting to resent those in your life.

Why is it so hard to stop?

Because for many, ‘people pleasing’ is the nervous system’s deeply ingrained way of staying safe, connected and accepted - and it shows up in your body well before it enters your conscious mind. That's why common clinical and cognitive remedies like “just say no” aren’t always enough

It’s about learning to catch your hyper vigilance, hold yourself with the fear long enough to recognise what’s actually true for you - and then building the capacity to act on it.

Beyond Boundaries: The Missing Piece in Healing People Pleasing.

“People Pleasing” is the name we’ve all adopted for what is fundamentally a stress response – known as “Fawning”. It lives side by side the other more commonly known stress responses - fight, flight and freeze.

The fundamental function of fawning is to sacrifice your own needs for the sake of the other.

Notice Yourself

“You can't honour a need you can't feel.”

Outcome: Reconnect with your body and begin recognising your own needs, wants, preferences and internal signals - rather than automatically overriding them or tuning them out.

What’s included: Explore interoception, bodily cues, preferences and the subtle moments where you abandon yourself. You’ll begin learning to notice what’s happening inside you with curiosity, distinguish thoughts from sensations and feelings, and gather information from your body before deciding what to do with it.

Week 1Somatics & Interoception

Stay With Yourself

“I can feel uncomfortable without abandoning myself.”

Outcome: Learn to stay connected to yourself through uncomfortable feelings without automatically appeasing, fixing or reverting to people-pleasing.

What’s included: Understand the relief cycle that keeps people-pleasing going, learn the difference between feeling unsafe and actually being unsafe, and practise staying with difficult emotions rather than immediately acting to make them disappear. You’ll explore guilt, shame and self-soothing while building capacity to tolerate the discomfort of choosing yourself.

Week 2Discomfort Is Not the Same as Danger

Choose Yourself

“The cost of connection should never be self-abandonment.”

Outcome: Turn self-awareness into action. Practise expressing your needs, preferences and boundaries while learning to tolerate disappointment, conflict and the possibility that choosing yourself may change a relationship.

What’s included: Explore boundaries, anger, resentment, attachment-style specific people pleasing traits, and the fears underneath your difficulty saying no. You’ll learn the difference between a request and a boundary, practise micro-No’s and begin translating your internal signals into honest communication and action - without abandoning yourself to preserve connection.

Week 3Beyond Boundaries

Become Yourself

“Who am I when I stop organising myself around everyone else?”

Outcome: Begin discovering who you are beyond the people-pleasing - what you actually want, value, enjoy and stand for - and create more space for a life that reflects your authentic Yes.

What’s included: Explore desire, identity, values, strengths, joy and the parts of yourself that may have been shaped around keeping others happy. You’ll begin building a clearer sense of what you want, what you stand for and how you want to live - while developing greater self-trust and permission to make mistakes, change your mind and be fully yourself.

Week 4Identity, Values & Authenticity

My Story and How We Got Here…

Hi, I’m Tess, a somatic psychotherapist based in Adelaide, and the founder of The Ember Room. For nearly two years, I’ve worked in private practice with a diverse range of individual and couples clients, supporting people to better understand themselves, their relationships, their emotions and their needs.

Last November I recognised that towards the end of the year, I often find myself exhausted and ready for a long nap, as my Celtic DNA yearns for winter around Christmas time… yet it is the time when our hemisphere is waking up and getting ready for silly season and the peak of social summer.

I took myself through a month of deep self preservation and rest and called it ‘NOvember’. Where I gave myself permission for just one month to say no to absolutely everything and anything that wasn’t 100% obligatory and didn’t nourish my nervous system and general well being. I had an incredible experience, with all my health stats subsequently shooting through the roof, and went into the Christmas period the most resourced I can ever recall as an adult.

Meanwhile, the theme of People Pleasing was coming up time and time again with clients. I found myself constantly recognising the same foundational pattern: that people pleasing starts as a fawning stress response, and requires someone to deny and suppress their own interoceptive responses to personal distress in order to prioritise connection and safety.

Where other methodologies focus on cognitive concepts like boundaries and simply learning to say no, I saw the complexity of why people can’t assert their needs - because they’re often so disconnected from themselves they have no idea what those needs even are.

So, I started dreaming up the idea to open NOvember to the public this year as a cute 4 week campaign to encourage people to say no and share about it. Alas, I couldn’t help myself, and it turned into a powerful and potent 4 week course that guides and supports people through essential teachings and practices to help them understand and start shifting their people pleasing tendencies.

I am incredibly proud of what I’ve created and so look forward to helping you learn, and shift these long standing habits to find access to your no, so that you can fully live your yes.

Tess xo
