It's a plain-English starting point, written for a small health service in South Australia. It isn't legal advice and it doesn't claim the practice meets every legal requirement. Every item marked "Confirm" needs a real answer before this page goes live.

Who I am

The Ember Room is the counselling and somatic therapy practice of Tess Fowler, at 43 Edward Street, Norwood SA 5067, with online sessions across Australia. In this policy, "I", "me" and "my" mean Tess Fowler and The Ember Room. Confirm: legal business name and ABN

Because I provide a health service, the information I hold about you includes health information. Under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), health information is sensitive information, and it gets extra protection. The Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) apply to health service providers whatever the size of the business, so the small business exemption doesn't apply to me.

This policy explains what I collect, why, how I look after it, and what you can ask of me.

How to contact me

For any privacy question, request or complaint, email me at hello@tessfowler.com.au. Confirm: postal address for privacy requests, if different

What I collect, and how

What I collect

  • Contact details: your name, email, phone number and how you'd like me to contact you.
  • Health information you share: what you tell me in sessions, in the free call, by email or in forms, such as your background, what's bringing you to therapy and how you're going.
  • Session notes: brief notes I write after sessions so your care stays consistent.
  • Intake form answers: the details you give me before your first session. Confirm: intake form fields (e.g. date of birth, emergency contact, GP)
  • Payment details: records of what you've paid, invoices, and your health fund details if you claim a Bupa rebate. Confirm: how payments are taken and whether any card details are stored

In couples sessions, I collect information from both people. The contact form on this website asks only for your name, email and preferred contact method, so please leave health details out of it.

Where it's practical, you can deal with me without giving your full name, for example when asking a general question. For ongoing therapy I do need to know who you are.

How I collect it

I collect information directly from you wherever I can, through:

  • the online booking platform, when you book a call or session Confirm: booking platform (currently Calendly; Halaxy or Cliniko if moving)
  • my intake form Confirm: intake form platform (currently Google Forms)
  • email, phone and the free 15-minute call
  • sessions, in person or online Confirm: video platform, and that sessions are never recorded
  • other health professionals, such as a referring GP, but only with your consent.

Why I collect it, and who sees it

Why I collect it and how I use it

  • to provide counselling, somatic therapy and Guided Rest sessions
  • to arrange, confirm and change appointments
  • to keep notes so I can support you well over time
  • to take payments and help you claim health fund rebates
  • to reply to your enquiries
  • to meet my legal and professional obligations Confirm: professional body and membership (e.g. ACA)
  • to send emails you've signed up for, such as NOvember letters. You can unsubscribe at any time.

I don't sell your information, and I don't use your health information for marketing.

Who I share it with

What you share with me stays confidential. I only share your information:

  • with your consent, for example with your GP or another health professional, or with Bupa when you make a claim
  • where the law requires or permits it, for example if I believe there's a serious threat to your life, health or safety or someone else's, or if a court order or subpoena requires it Confirm: any mandatory reporting obligations that apply
  • in clinical supervision, where I discuss my work with a supervisor to keep my practice safe and ethical. I remove your name and identifying details first. Confirm: supervision arrangement and de-identification practice
  • with service providers who run the systems I use for booking, forms, email and storage. They handle data on my behalf.

Some of those providers may store data outside Australia. Confirm: which providers store data overseas, and in which countries

Website and analytics

This website doesn't use advertising pixels, retargeting or social media tracking. I use cookieless analytics to count page visits and button clicks, such as when someone taps a "Book" link. It doesn't identify you and doesn't record anything you type into forms. Confirm: analytics tool (e.g. Plausible or Fathom)

Access, corrections and complaints

Accessing and correcting your information

You can ask for a copy of the information I hold about you, or ask me to correct anything that's wrong or out of date. Email me and I'll respond within 30 days. Confirm: response time, and whether any fee applies for copies

In rare cases the law allows me to refuse access, for example where giving it would put someone's safety at serious risk. If that happens, I'll explain why in writing.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, please tell me first at hello@tessfowler.com.au. I'll take it seriously and reply in writing. Confirm: complaint response time

If you're not satisfied with my response, you can contact:

  • the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC): oaic.gov.au
  • the SA Health and Community Services Complaints Commissioner: hcscc.sa.gov.au
