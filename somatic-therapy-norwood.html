Working with the body as well as the words

Most therapy happens in words. That matters, and we'll talk plenty. But many of the patterns that bring people to therapy, like going quiet when someone is upset with you or saying yes before you've had a chance to think, happen in the body before they reach the mind.

As a somatic therapist, I combine body-based "bottom-up" approaches with cognitive "top-down" practices to get to the heart of what's truly going on for you.

In practice, that might mean pausing to notice your breath, where tension sits, or what happens in your chest when a particular person comes up. Nothing is done to you. You're in control the whole time, and you can always say no.

We don't view anxiety or depression as a disorder that must be "fixed". Instead, we build your capacity to sit with uncomfortable feelings long enough to listen to the wisdom beneath. From there, I support you to take more agency over your life and your choices.

Before you book

I've never had therapy. Is that okay?

Yes. Many people who come to me are new to therapy, or have tried talk-only therapy and felt something was missing. The free 15-minute call is there for exactly this: to ask questions before you commit to anything.

What if I don't know what to talk about?

That's common, and it's fine. We can start with whatever brought you to look for help, even if it's just a feeling that something isn't right. The body often has a clearer answer than the mind does.

Will I have to do anything physical?

Mostly we sit and talk. I may invite you to notice your breath, posture or sensations as you speak. Every invitation is optional, and you can say no at any point.

Does somatic therapy work online?

Yes. Sessions run on Zoom. Confirm: how Tess adapts somatic work for video Confirm: whether online clients can be anywhere in Australia, and that online fees are the same

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on what you're working on. Some people come for a focused stretch; others prefer longer-term work. We'll check in together about how it's going, and you're never locked in.

Is it confidential?

Yes. What you share stays between us, within the legal limits I'll explain in our first session. See the privacy policy for how your information is stored.
