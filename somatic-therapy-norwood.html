Somatic Therapy

As a somatic therapist, I use predominantly body based “bottom-up” approaches combined with cognitive “top-down” practices, to get to the heart of what’s truly going on for you.

This happens through a blend of different modalities (IFS, CBT, EFT, Narrative, Grief work, Somatic Experiencing).

No session is the same

Therapy looks different for everyone and no session is the same. That said – core themes do come up regularly.

Themes of wanting to love ourselves better, love others better, a perceived lack of emotional safety based on old experiences, fear of being unlovable, fear of getting things wrong, fear of feeling too much, being too much and so on.

Beliefs from our childhood, adolescence and beyond are embedded in our unconscious and dictate our behaviour and relationship to ourselves and to others.

In my sessions, we don’t view anxiety or depression as a disorder that must be ‘fixed’. The uncomfortable feelings and sensations that come with emotion are not treated as something to avoid, instead we increase your capacity to sit with the discomfort long enough to listen to the wisdom beneath, and allow the body and mind to connect and truly communicate as one.

With greater curiosity

Asking different parts of ourselves what they might be trying to say, what they might be needing, and learning to dive inwards to understand our motivations and behaviours a little better with greater curiosity and self compassion.

From here I support you to take more agency over your life and empower your choices and behaviours moving forward for genuine lasting change.

Psychotherapy

Psychotherapy explores the unconscious processes, life experiences, and relational patterns that shape a person’s current behavior and emotional state. It aims to uncover suppressed emotions, unresolved conflicts, and inner drives that influence a person's wellbeing.

Somatic Therapy

Somatic Therapy acknowledges that emotions and trauma can be stored in the body, affecting a person’s physical health and well-being.

Who is this for?

Anyone who needs space and safety to slow down and listen to their inner wisdom.

Building emotional capacity to be with our direct experience increases our threshold for the complexities and challenges of life.

Through sharing your stories, and tapping into unmet emotions (often such as fear, anger, or sadness) we can help you cultivate deeper ease and autonomy in life.
